Title

Kriya Yoga (Paramahansa Yogananda, YSS): Lineage, Lessons and the Path to Initiation

Description

Kriya Yoga, as taught by Paramahansa Yogananda through the Yogoda Satsanga Society of India (YSS), is the tradition behind Autobiography of a Yogi (1946). This video traces its history as the tradition tells it: lost for centuries, reintroduced by Mahavatar Babaji, then passed through Lahiri Mahasaya (1828–1895) and Swami Sri Yukteswar (1855–1936) to Yogananda, who founded YSS in 1917 and Self-Realization Fellowship in 1920. You'll see how the path works today. Students enrol in 18 basic home-study Lessons that arrive by post every fortnight. With Lesson 17, after roughly a year of preparation, comes the invitation to apply for Kriya Yoga. The basic series is a nominal ₹1,600 in India, and Lessons students are offered online meditations, classes and retreats. The video also covers the headquarters at Dakshineswar, near Kolkata (with a branch headquarters in Ranchi), how YSS views the living guru, and where the guide's FAQ mentions Kriya Yoga. This video summarises the guide "Meditation Techniques from India" at https://harsh.in/meditation-techniques-from-india/.
